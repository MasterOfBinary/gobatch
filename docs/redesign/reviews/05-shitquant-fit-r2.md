# Review r2: consumer fit of PROPOSAL.md revision 2 (sections 5.1, 5.4, 5.6, 6)

Revision 2 fixes every misread of the architecture named in r1: the normalizer is network-free, the pending map is a `Promise`, ordering is by readiness not first dependency, 6.1 uses per-kind classifiers and abort as the normal end, 6.3 admits group commit is a `Memory` API change, and the delivery plan defers ShitQuant to a measurement. What remains is one design-level hole in 6.2 and a handful of accuracy slips. Ranked.

## R1. 6.2 still breaks byte-identical replay: the admit worker and the reader both call `Recorder.Admit` (design-level)

6.2 routes only swap→trade admission through the `admit` kind, whose handler runs on the kind's worker goroutine (5.5 "batchers pull ready tasks from the scheduler"; 5.9 table: ready→building→in handler is "the kind's worker"). The reader goroutine keeps admitting coverage, finality and retraction facts directly, and 6.2 says the finality and coverage maps "stay" there.

Today `block()` admits the held swaps and *then* calls `blockTime` (`normalizer.go:426-435`), which may `emitComplete` coverage (`coverage.go:284`, `:426-449`) and `closeInterruption` (`:457-488`). Under 6.2, `Resolve(slotKey)` readies the admit tasks, the reader proceeds at once to `blockTime`, and the coverage `Admit` from the reader races the trade `Admit`s on the worker for the recorder's lock (`recorder.go:302-304`). Sequence order between trades and coverage becomes scheduling-dependent. Acceptance (1) (`normalization.md:245`) fails and "sequence ... assigned at one serialized point, never by whichever goroutine received the message" (DESIGN.md Principles) is broken in exactly the way it forbids.

Same race on data, not just order: the admit handler writes `n.markets`, `n.occurrences`, `n.signatures`, `run.admitted` (`normalizer.go:456-475`, `:504-508`, `coverage.go:223-231`) and reads `n.finalized`/`n.entries` in `applyKnown` (`finality.go:86-100`), while the reader's `statuses`/`finalBlock` write `n.entries`, `n.finalized`, `n.waiting` and read `n.occurrences` (`finality.go:108-127`, `:213-224`). No lock today (`normalizer.go:70-72`).

The only correct shape is every `Admit` behind the one `Workers: 1` kind, with block/statuses/slot/control records registered as dependency-free tasks in record order, which puts the whole normalizer inside the handler. That is a channel to one goroutine, and it is what `liveFollower.Run` already is (`live.go:399-454`). So the claim "readiness order equal to completing-record order because everything is driven from the one reader goroutine" is true only of the admit kind's internal order, and 6.2 should say the join can be expressed but cannot be adopted piecemeal. "Adopt only if a measurement asks, and then for the join alone" is therefore not an option; it is the broken option.

Within the admit kind, the readiness-order claim does hold for held swaps: `Resolve(S)` is one event, tasks ready in registration order = notification record order and swap order within a record (`normalizer.go:336-340`, `:372`), and a late swap's `After` on a resolved handle is its own event at its record position. That matches today's `state.swaps` order and `swap()`'s immediate admit.

## R2. 6.2 semantics that do not match today (accuracy)

- **block_conflict is over-counted.** `if !blocks.Resolve(...) { count(block_conflict) }` counts every second block. Today a second block with the *same* blockhash is silently ignored and only a different blockhash counts (`normalizer.go:399-407`). The consumer must peek the resolved value (`Handle(key).Result` from the reader is allowed by 5.8) before counting.
- **`Pending()` is not reproducible from `Stats`.** `live.go:476-481` prints slots, held swaps and blocks-with-no-swaps (`normalizer.go:234-245`). `Stats.Pending` per kind gives held swaps; "blocks awaiting swaps" are resolved promise keys nobody registered against, which nothing exposes. 6.2's "removes one map and one method pair" undercounts what is rebuilt.
- **The promise value is not `Block`.** `CompletingReceipt` and provenance need the block record's receipt and `(capture_id, sequence)` (`normalizer.go:409-411`, `:449-454`); `solana.Block` carries neither. The promise must hold today's `slotState{block, receipt, ref}` triple. Minor, but it is the same struct being moved, not removed.
- Correct as written: `time_unknown` as a build error counted by the observer (today `normalizer.go:377-381`, `:416-424`); late notification ready at registration; market→trade→finality inside one handler; replay availability from the completing record if `pair` computes it from both receipts.

## R3. 6.1 "two in flight per kind" is not today's rule, and priority is silently dropped (accuracy)

Today the cap is two in flight *across all three kinds* (`scheduler.go:405` `inFlightCount < cfg.InFlight`; `raw-capture.md:67` "at most two requests in flight"), and confirmed fetches take capacity before batches before finalized (`scheduler.go:256-296`). `Workers: 2` on three kinds is six in flight with no priority; section 7 drops cross-kind capacity as a non-goal. 6.1 lists the rate limiter moving into the RPC client but not the in-flight cap or the priority; both would have to move there too (a semaphore with priority in the client), and 6.1 should say the priority ordering is lost rather than imply it is reproduced.

Other 6.1 items check out: `MinWait: 200ms, MaxItems: 256` batches, and 6.1 correctly says slot/first-seen order in the envelope (`scheduler.go:469-493`, `request.go:277-281`) is lost; abort as the normal end with the consumer distinguishing duration from SIGINT matches `request.go:72-74` and `source.go:378-386`; per-kind classifiers match `request.go:238-248`; `Remember` reproduces `slotFor` (`scheduler.go:500-512`). Two residual gaps: (a) group budget is still per task (5.7) while today a batch's budget is anchored at its earliest signature (`scheduler.go:438-442`, `raw-capture.md:70`); 5.7 does not say whose budget ends a group retry. (b) `statuses` dedup by `sigKey` is global, today's `seenSig` is per slot (`scheduler.go:123`); harmless to the dataset since `finalizedStatus` acts on every occurrence of a signature (`finality.go:213-224`), but the journal differs. Also `Submit` on the reader goroutine must never block (`scheduler.go:144-151`); 6.1 sets no `MaxOpen`, and 5.5 should state that unset means unbounded, and that `After` tasks under only a watermark have no root and are outside `MaxOpen` anyway.

## R4. Remaining misreads, with locations

1. Section 6 intro: "the only measurement on file says keep per-record commits." That measurement is the phase 2 *capture journal* (`mvp-phases.md:54`: 3.42 records/s, mean 10.45 ms). The admission-log measurement 6.3 relies on was written but never run (`mvp-phases.md:79`: "no measurement exists to record"). Say "capture journal" and "none yet for the admission log".
2. 6.2 `Policy{MaxItems: 1}` on admit: fine, but the claim of equality with today also needs `Workers` unset (1); say so, since 5.6 makes determinism conditional on it.
3. 6.4: "many per-market evaluations submitted when a cutoff watermark advances, fanning in to one admit kind with one worker". `mvp-phases.md:118-120` says rules read a `market.View` at saved cutoffs and record cutoffs and decisions "through the same admission-log path"; it names no per-market fan-out, no watermark, and the backtest runner drives the historical view (no wall clock). Not inconsistent, but 6.4 describes a design that does not exist; keep the "design document first" sentence and drop the shape.
4. 5.4 `Watermark.Wait` vs `Journal.Wait`: correctly caveated now ("does not model closed"); note `reader.go:45-56` also needs the closed check *before* waiting, so the sentinel is not a full substitute either.

## R5. Adoption order

"6.4, then 6.2, never 6.1": 6.4 first is right and matches `mvp-phases.md:116` (ticker first, GoBatch only if the flush measures badly). "Never 6.1" is right. "Then 6.2" does not hold as drawn (R1): the join alone cannot be adopted without moving every `Admit` behind the kind, and once that is done the workflow is a one-goroutine channel the code already has. Either state 6.2 as "expressible, not adoptable in part" or move it to "never". The phase 6 runner is the only remaining candidate for `workflow`, and only after its design exists.

## Bottom line

r2 is honest where r1 was not; the architecture picture at the top of section 6 is now correct. Fix R1 (the interleaving), R2's block_conflict count, and R3's in-flight/priority statement, and change 6.2's recommendation from "for the join alone" to "not in part". The delivery plan's "nothing in ShitQuant until a measurement asks" is the right sentence and should stay.
